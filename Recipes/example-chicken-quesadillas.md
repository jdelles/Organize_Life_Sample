# Chicken Quesadillas

**Tags:** weeknight-ok, kid-friendly
**Prep time:** 10 min
**Cook time:** 15 min
**Total time:** 25 min
**Servings:** 4
**Last made:** [DATE]

---

## Ingredients

*Scaled for 4 servings. Adjust as needed.*

- 1.5 lbs boneless chicken breast or thighs
- 8 medium flour tortillas
- 1.5 cups shredded Mexican blend cheese
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 cup frozen or fresh corn
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Olive oil or cooking spray
- Serve with: salsa, sour cream, guacamole, hot sauce

---

## Instructions

1. Season chicken with cumin, chili powder, salt, and pepper. Cook in a skillet over medium-high heat until cooked through (about 6-7 min per side). Rest 5 min, then slice or shred.
2. While chicken rests, sauté bell pepper in the same pan for 3-4 min until softened. Add corn and beans, stir to warm through.
3. Heat a clean skillet or griddle over medium heat. Spray lightly with oil.
4. Lay one tortilla flat. Sprinkle cheese on one half, add chicken and veggie mixture, fold over.
5. Cook 2-3 min per side until golden and cheese is melted. Repeat for remaining quesadillas.
6. Slice into wedges and serve with toppings.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

Filling stores well in fridge for 3-4 days. Make the filling ahead (Sunday batch cook) and assemble + cook quesadillas fresh in 10 min on busy nights.

## Notes / Variations

- Swap chicken for leftover rotisserie chicken to cut cook time in half
- Add spinach or diced zucchini to the filling for extra veg
- For a lower-carb version, use whole wheat or low-carb tortillas
