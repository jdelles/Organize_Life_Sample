# LinkedIn Learning Log
Last updated: [DATE]

This file feeds the LinkedIn content generator. Add entries whenever you:
- Learn something interesting in your field
- Ship something or solve a hard problem at work
- Read an article or paper worth sharing a take on
- Have a strong opinion about something in the industry
- Experience something in practice that differs from what people write about online

The content generator will NOT write fluff. It reads this log and generates post ideas grounded in what you've actually experienced.

---

## How to add an entry

Just paste a new section at the top (newest first) in this format:

```
## [Date] — [Short title]
What happened / what you learned / what you built:
[1-3 sentences in your own words, as rough as you want]

Any strong take or reaction:
[Optional — what surprised you, what you'd push back on, what was counterintuitive]
```
